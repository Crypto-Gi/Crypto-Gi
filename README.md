I'm a **Senior Systems Engineer and Consulting Architect at HPE**, **CCIE Data Center certified**, co-founder of **Mirasko Solutions**, and based in the Chicago area. I've spent 15+ years in network security, from data-center firewalls in Doha to leading pre-sales strategy for one of HPE Aruba's largest SD-WAN transformations (20k+ sites).

I turn complex requirements into secure, reliable networks, then explain the risk in plain language: **Asset → Threat → Impact.** If a risk isn't clear in ten minutes, it won't get managed.

Nights and weekends, I build with AI: agents, MCP servers and search pipelines that take the repetitive parts of this job off my plate.

> *The network moves the data. The AI finds the meaning. The human decides what matters.*
>
> I don't think the goal is to out-think the machine. It's to use it to buy back time, and spend that time on people, judgment and things worth building.
